=====================================================
SECTION 1: INTERACTIVE DOT BACKGROUND (HERO)
=====================================================
Purpose: the dots must be clearly visible at a glance, and visitors must be able to play with them. They are not dust.

- One full-width HTML canvas behind the hero content (z-index below all content). Only the hero area responds to the pointer; links and buttons remain fully clickable.
- Counts: desktop 160 to 200 dots, tablet 100 to 120, mobile 70 to 90.
- Three depth layers:
  - Back: 2px, 25% to 35% opacity, slowest
  - Middle: 3px, 40% to 55% opacity
  - Front: 4 to 5px, 60% to 75% opacity, fastest
- Colour: most dots #111 to #1E1E1E. About one in twelve is teal #00806A. Solid dots, no blur.
- Idle: each dot drifts in its own slow loop (2 to 5px), so the field is alive without the cursor.
- Cursor: dots within about 150px are pushed away, closest pushed hardest. When the cursor stops, dots spring back over about 1.5 seconds with an elastic ease-out curve. Dots near the cursor grow up to 30% and darken slightly.
- Fast movement leaves a short soft trail of dots that fades.
- Constellation lines: when two dots are within about 110px, draw a hairline at 12% to 18% opacity. Desktop only.
- Click or tap: a soft ring ripples out over about 0.8 seconds, and nearby dots burst outward and settle back.
- Press and hold: dots are attracted to the pointer and form a small cluster. Release sends them back with the spring motion.
- Touch: a finger drag works like the cursor, and a tap works like a click.
- Performance: one canvas, capped at 60fps, devicePixelRatio capped at 2, paused when the hero is off-screen or the tab is hidden.
- Reduced motion: show the same dots as a still pattern with no movement, lines, or ripples.
- Dots sit behind text but never above buttons, the status card, or form fields. Keep any dot behind text under 40% opacity.

Acceptance: at normal viewing distance the dots are obvious; moving the cursor visibly pushes them and they return smoothly; a click creates a visible ripple; all links and buttons still work.
