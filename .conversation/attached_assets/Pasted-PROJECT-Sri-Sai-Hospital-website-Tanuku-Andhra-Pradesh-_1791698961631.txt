INTERACTIVE BACKGROUND (must have)
- A field of small BLACK dots (#111 to #222 at 10–35% opacity, 1.5–3px) on the light hero background. They drift slowly on their own.
- When the cursor moves near them, dots gently scatter away from it and ease back to their home positions over about 1.5 seconds. Use three depth layers that move at different speeds.
- Implement as one HTML canvas, with about 60 dots on desktop and 30 on mobile. Pause when the hero is off-screen or the tab is hidden.
- On touch devices, dots respond to finger drag.
- If reduced motion is enabled, show a static dot pattern.
- Keep dots out of the text, buttons, and status card. Clear them from behind the headline.

MOTION AND POLISH (all of these)

- Buttons have a subtle magnetic pull toward the cursor on desktop, and a clear keyboard focus state
- Cards lift with a soft shadow on hover
- Sticky header that condenses and gains a soft background blur after scrolling
- Floating action bar on mobile with Call and WhatsApp
- A thin progress line at the top of the page that shows how far the user has scrolled
- Use no more than three motion patterns in total, so the site stays calm
